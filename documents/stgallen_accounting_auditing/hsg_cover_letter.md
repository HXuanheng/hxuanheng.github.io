I am writing to apply for the open-rank Professorship in Accounting and Auditing at the School of Management, University of St. Gallen. I would like to be considered at the **Assistant Professor (tenure track)** level.

I am a final-stage PhD candidate in Accounting at Bocconi University, advised by Peter Pope, and a recent Visiting Scholar at Columbia Business School under the sponsorship of Stephen Penman. My research programme studies how accounting information supports valuation across the full set of firm stakeholders — equity investors, debtholders, M&A participants, and supply-chain partners — using primarily empirical-archival methods, complemented by AI-based text analysis. This agenda aligns directly with HSG's emphasis on Management Accounting and Financial Leadership, including digitalisation, artificial intelligence, and financial leadership as substantive priorities.

My solo-authored job market paper, *Accounting-Based Monitoring: Evidence from Supply Chain Contracts*, hand-collects material supply agreements filed with the SEC between 2000 and 2023 and shows that accounting-based monitoring is a pervasive contractual mechanism: 31% of contracts include such clauses, with customers holding verification rights in over 70% of monitored relationships. I document that intensive monitoring improves operating efficiency, reduces cost volatility, and shortens cash-conversion cycles along supply chains. The paper speaks to current debates in auditing, internal control over inter-organisational relationships, and management accounting — areas central to the HSG appointment. Two further papers extend the agenda: *Are Disclosed Merger Synergies Realized?* (with Atif Ellahie, Irem Tuna, and Roberto Vincenzi), in final revision for journal submission, examines disclosure credibility in over 12,000 U.S. M&A transactions; and *Leverage and Operating Risk in Asset Pricing: An Accounting Perspective* (with Peter Pope) develops accounting-based factor measures that subsume the Fama–French six-factor model and outperform the q-factor model. A new project with Peter Pope on characteristic-based factors and macroeconomic risk is under way.

On the teaching side, I am currently the lecturer for *Financial Statement Analysis for Company Valuation* at Bocconi (97 master's students, 2026), where I have designed the course around an explicit integration of canonical FSA frameworks with AI-augmented research workflows. I have served as Teaching Assistant since 2023 across three Bocconi courses on FSA, accounting, and valuation, and contributed to the Penman–Pope textbook on FSA for value investing. I am able to teach in **English from day one** at HSG, in courses covering financial statement analysis, valuation, management accounting, auditing, and AI in financial analysis at master's, MACFin, doctoral, and executive-education levels. **I am committed to reaching teaching proficiency in German within the two-year window specified in the call**, and I will begin structured German study from the moment my candidacy is shortlisted.

I have presented at major accounting conferences — including the EAA Annual Conference, the Lancaster Accounting Research & Policy Symposium, the Hawaii Accounting Research Conference, Rutgers, the Padua–Bolzano Early Researcher Consortium, the UC3M International Accounting Symposium, Columbia Business School, and the University of Manchester — and I serve as an Ad Hoc Reviewer for *The Journal of Business Finance & Accounting*. I would welcome the opportunity to extend this network through the School of Management and to contribute to the academic self-administration of the Department in due course.
